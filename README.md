# Your personal trainer, in ChatGPT or Claude

[Русская версия](README.ru.md) · [Step-by-step setup](START-HERE.md)

Turn your usual AI chat into a place to plan exercise, follow your program, and keep track of progress. The coach asks about your goals, experience, schedule, and equipment, then helps you agree on a realistic first week. Food and weight tracking are optional.

**New to ChatGPT? Open [START-HERE.md](START-HERE.md).** It explains every step, including choosing a model, finding the downloaded files, starting the conversation, and saving your progress. You do not need coding knowledge, a GitHub account, or a skill installer.

## What you will set up

You will create a private **My Personal Trainer** project in your existing app, paste a short set of instructions, and add two files from the `starter` folder:

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| [COACH-HANDBOOK.md](starter/COACH-HANDBOOK.md) | Gives ChatGPT the coaching methods and research. You do not need to read it yourself. |
| [MY-TRAINER.md](starter/MY-TRAINER.md) | Starts blank and becomes your private profile, agreed plan, and training diary. |

For Claude, use [starter/CLAUDE.md](starter/CLAUDE.md) as the coaching file instead. Both apps use [PROJECT-INSTRUCTIONS.txt](starter/PROJECT-INSTRUCTIONS.txt) in the project's instructions field. Start with your app's default model; the setup guide explains when deeper reasoning may help.

## Everyday use

Speak naturally: “What is today's workout?”, “I finished three sets of eight”, or “Review my week.” The coach keeps agreed plans, things you actually did, and proposed changes separate. If you already have a program, ask it to help you follow that program exactly.

At the end, ask for the **complete updated MY-TRAINER.md**, save it privately, and replace the older project copy. Returning to the same project helps keep the right context available. See [saving your progress](docs/SAVING-YOUR-PROGRESS.md).

Share this repository link to give someone a fresh starter. Keep your filled-in notebook and personal project private. The download contains generic guidance and blank records; your conversation takes place with your AI provider. See [privacy](docs/PRIVACY.md).
